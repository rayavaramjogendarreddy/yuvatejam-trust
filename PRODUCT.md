# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 15 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Vercel

## Users

Primary users are donors seeking transparent charitable contributions, volunteers applying for field initiatives, community members seeking helpline/relief support, and corporate CSR partners evaluating NGO credibility.

## Product Purpose

Provide transparent information on charitable programs (Education, Healthcare, Women Empowerment, Child Welfare, Environment, Elderly Care, Disaster Relief), process tax-deductible contributions, onboard field volunteers, and provide direct emergency contact helplines.

## Positioning

A 100% transparent, registered public charitable trust (Reg. No. 124/2012) delivering direct grassroots impact with zero administrative deduction on direct relief funds.

## Operating Context

Web platform optimized for desktop and mobile visitors, offering quick donation options, volunteer application forms, field gallery showcases, and emergency helpline contact channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities: Dynamic static route generation for 37+ pages, AST HTML content parsing, contact and volunteer form handling, interactive lightbox image gallery, responsive layout system.
- Constraints: Must preserve all original registration details (Reg. No. 124/2012), official SBI bank account details, existing contact phone numbers/addresses, and SEO structured data.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: YUVATEJAM TRUST
- Registration: Reg. No. 124/2012 (Registered Public Charitable Trust)
- Visual Identity: Trustworthy emerald/teal & warm slate color palette, clean typography, authentic human & community field imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real program descriptions and dynamic page content for 37+ initiative routes (`src/lib/data/pages-data.ts`).
- Official bank donation details (State Bank of India).
- Active volunteer application form structure (`src/components/forms/VolunteerForm.tsx`).
- Active contact form structure (`src/components/forms/ContactForm.tsx`).

## Product Principles

1. **Absolute Transparency**: Clear registration numbers, direct bank account details, and zero hidden fees.
2. **Human & Authentic**: Prioritize real community field photography and beneficiary impact stories over abstract generic shapes.
3. **Action-Oriented**: Clear, accessible pathways for visitors to donate, volunteer, or reach emergency helplines.
4. **Editorial Clarity**: High-contrast, scannable visual hierarchy with distinct section patterns instead of repetitive boxed cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance, semantic HTML5 structure, responsive touch targets (min 44x44px), high contrast ratios, explicit image `alt` attributes, and `prefers-reduced-motion` motion support.
